**Status meanings**
- `draft` — being written; don't build from it.
- `ready` — the REQ's ready-to-build checklist is complete; safe to build.
- `in-progress` — being implemented.
- `done` — deployed to production **and** acceptance criteria verified there. Merged-but-not-deployed is still `in-progress`.
- `dropped` — not doing; reason in the file.
